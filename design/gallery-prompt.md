# Gallery background

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Production asset: `public/media/gallery.webp`; original: `design/gallery-original.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background plate for an editorial cinematic website, landscape 1920x1080 or similar wide aspect. Create an original minimalist warm contemporary art gallery interior photographed at eye level with architectural depth. Pale travertine floor, warm ivory plaster walls, a large softly shadowed architectural opening on right leading into a distant sunlit room, dark bronze glass framing near the extreme right edge, restrained stone plinth at distant right. Left 50 percent deliberately quiet pale wall with soft natural shadow, suitable for dark editorial website text. Clear foreground/middle/distant planes, generous empty central space for a floating phone UI to be overlaid in code. Warm daylight from upper left, subtle realistic texture, premium architectural photography, cinematic but airy and luminous. No people, no phone, no interface, no letters, no text, no watermarks. Avoid ornate decor or clutter. Background plate only.

The same background plate is used on three softly masked DOM planes with different scroll travel. This is a layered 2D camera treatment, not a reconstructed 3D room.
